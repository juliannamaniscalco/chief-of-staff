# Morning Workout Plan — 20 Minutes During First Nap

**When:** 9-9:20am, Monday-Friday, before you open Slack  
**Where:** Peloton (bike or app)  
**Goal:** Build the habit first, intensity second

---

## Week 1-2: Foundation

The goal is showing up, not crushing it. Pick classes that feel manageable. Let Peloton do the programming — you just press play.

### Daily Structure

| Day | Class Type | Duration | Where to Find |
|-----|------------|----------|---------------|
| Mon | Strength (Full Body or Lower Body) | 20 min | Strength → Full Body or Lower Body |
| Tue | Strength (Upper Body or Core) | 20 min | Strength → Upper Body or Core |
| Wed | Low Impact Ride or Walk | 20 min | Cycling → Low Impact or Walking |
| Thu | Strength (Full Body or Core) | 20 min | Strength → Full Body or Core |
| Fri | Yoga or Stretch | 20 min | Yoga → Beginner or Slow Flow |

### Recommended Instructors for Postpartum/Beginner
- **Strength:** Robin Arzón, Adrian Williams, Andy Speer
- **Low Impact:** Christine D'Ercole, Hannah Corbin
- **Yoga:** Aditi Shah, Kristin McGee

### Week 1 Goal
Just complete 5 classes. Any 5. Doesn't matter which ones.

### Week 2 Goal
Complete 5 classes following the schedule above.

## Peloton Programs to Consider (After Week 4)

If you want more structure after building the habit:
- **Beginner Strength** — 2-3 weeks, foundational
- **Crush Your Core** — 4 weeks, 10 min/day (could add to your 20 min)
- **Total Strength** — 4 weeks, Andy Speer, more advanced

These stack classes for you. Zero decision-making.

## Rules

1. **If Lucianna wakes early:** A 10-min class counts. There are tons of them.
2. **If you miss a day:** Do NOT double up. Just do tomorrow's class.
3. **Pick the class the night before.** Bookmark it. Wake up knowing exactly what you're doing.
4. **Use the Peloton streak.** Let their gamification work for you.
5. **No phone scrolling between sets.** Stay in the class.
