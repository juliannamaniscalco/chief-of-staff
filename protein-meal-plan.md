# Protein-Focused Meal Plan

**Goal:** 100-120g protein/day, steady energy, supports breastfeeding  
**Approach:** Simple, repeatable meals. Batch cook on Sunday. Minimal daily decisions.

## Sunday Batch Prep (1-1.5 hours)

Do this while Lucianna naps or Mike has her:

1. **Egg muffins** — 12 count (25 min)
2. **Chicken** — grill or bake 2 lbs, portion (30 min)
3. **Hard boiled eggs** — 6-8 (15 min)
4. **Chop veggies** for the week (10 min)
5. **Portion snacks** into grab-and-go containers

That's it. Weekdays become assembly, not cooking.

---

## Rules

1. **Protein first.** Eat protein before carbs at each meal.
2. **Don't skip meals.** You're breastfeeding. Undereating tanks your energy and milk supply.
3. **Aim for 80%.** Perfection isn't the goal. Consistency is.
4. **Drink water.** At least 80oz/day. Keep a bottle at your desk.
5. **Flexible weekends.** Don't stress Saturday/Sunday. Just hit protein at dinner.

## Tracking (Optional)

If you want to track, use MyFitnessPal or Lose It for 1-2 weeks to see where you land. After that, you'll know portions intuitively.

If tracking feels like too much right now, just follow the plate rule:
- 1/2 plate = veggies
- 1/4 plate = protein (palm-sized)
- 1/4 plate = carbs (fist-sized)
