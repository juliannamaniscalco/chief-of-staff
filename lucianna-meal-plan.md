## Recipes

### Banana Cottage Cheese Muffins
Great for breakfast or snack. Freezer friendly.

**Ingredients:**
- 2 ripe bananas
- 1 egg
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup cottage cheese
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon

**Directions:**
1. Blend all ingredients
2. Pour into mini muffin tin
3. Bake at 350°F for 12-15 min

**Batch prep:** Makes ~24 mini muffins. Freeze and reheat as needed.

---

### Turkey Sweet Potato Patties
Protein-packed dinner. Freezer friendly.

**Ingredients:**
- 1/2 lb ground turkey
- 1/2 cup sweet potato (cooked and mashed)
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp breadcrumbs

**Directions:**
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Form into small patties
3. Bake at 400°F for 20 min (flip halfway)

**Batch prep:** Double or triple recipe. Freeze cooked patties. Reheat in microwave or oven.

---

### Spinach Banana Pancakes (Hulk Pancakes)
Hidden veggies! Freezer friendly.

**Ingredients:**
- 1 banana, mashed
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- Handful of spinach (blended with wet ingredients)

**Directions:**
1. Blend banana, egg, milk, vanilla, and spinach
2. Mix in flour and baking powder
3. Fry small pancakes on each side until golden

**Batch prep:** Make a big batch Sunday. Freeze flat, reheat in toaster.
